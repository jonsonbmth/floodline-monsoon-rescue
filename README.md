**FLOODLINE — Monsoon Rescue.** The river took the city. You are the only boat
left. Pilot a rescue boat through a drowned city over seven days of monsoon
rain: pull stranded survivors off rooftops, deliver them to the shelter,
dodge debris and whirlpools, refuel at the fuel barge — and on Day 7, outrun
the Surge, a dam-break wall of water crossing the city.

A complete original game in plain HTML5 Canvas + JavaScript, no libraries,
no network: 7 days with rising difficulty, upgrades between days, a boss
surge, combo scoring, a minimap, synthesized WebAudio sound (rain, engine,
thunder, sirens), pause, and win/lose/restart. Played with keyboard + mouse.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: WASD / arrows steer (or hold the mouse), SHIFT boost, E rescue,
P pause, M sound, R restart. Screenshots are staged with `?shot=start|play|boss|end&seed=N`
(see `shots.json`) — real renders of the game's own code.
